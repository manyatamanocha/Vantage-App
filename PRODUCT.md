# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Client-facing consultants in India, primarily early-career consultants with roughly three years of experience. They use AI tools already, but need help translating a specific, messy client ask into the right kind of AI approach.

## Product Purpose

Vantage helps a consultant understand a client problem, make a category-level AI recommendation, and practise the judgment required to make that decision again. Success is a clearer recommendation and a stronger first guess over time.

## Positioning

Vantage teaches durable AI-approach categories rather than recommending named products. Its defining mechanic is guess-before-reveal: the consultant commits to a category before seeing Vantage's recommendation.

## Operating Context

The core live workflow is: client ask → structured interpretation → category guess → comparative reveal → client takeaway → saved session summary. A daily practice case reuses the same guess-and-reveal learning loop.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The fixed approach taxonomy includes Classification, RAG, Prediction, Summarization, Generation, Extraction, Recommendation, and Anomaly Detection.
- Recommendations must remain category-level and may recommend only a general-purpose or specialized tool class, never a named product.
- The full flow preserves separate Handback and Session Summary screens, matching the confirmed wireframe structure.
- The current implementation uses Next.js, Supabase, Groq, Tailwind, and Shadcn.
- Avoid paid services and domain purchases.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Vantage.
- Voice: calm, plain-language, confidence-building, and non-technical without being beginner-oriented.
- Visual reference: the approved Vantage wireframe in the product vault.

## Product Principles

1. Make a messy ask usable before asking the consultant to judge it.
2. Build judgment through active recall, not passive answers.
3. Explain why the recommended category fits and plausible alternatives do not.
4. Keep the consultant in control with plain-language, client-ready outputs.
5. Preserve a coherent learning record across live work and practice.
